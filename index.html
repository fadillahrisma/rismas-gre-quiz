About this GRE Vocabulary Tool

Welcome to the GRE Vocabulary Builder, a project curated by Risma Fadillah. Preparing for the GRE Verbal Reasoning section requires a deep understanding of complex English vocabulary. This tool is designed to help students master 100 high-frequency GRE words through a gamified experience.

Whether you need an essential GRE word list or an interactive GRE vocabulary quiz, this app provides a seamless study experience. Challenge yourself, build your streak, and master the language of the GRE.
